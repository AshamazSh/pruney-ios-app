Pruney requires no login; tap Continue on first launch to grant full or limited Photos access, and use disposable, locally stored media for testing.
Swipe left to select deletion, right to keep, or up to resize; for Live Photos, the upward action selects Make static, and Keep all in the double-forward menu can finish the remaining review quickly.
Reviewing and configuring actions do not change files; Delete X or Start applies the selected actions subject to Photos system confirmation, with originals moved to Recently Deleted.
The free allowance is 50 deletions and 10 resize/Make static actions per run; exceeding either limit opens the non-consumable lifetime Pro offer (com.ashamazsh.pruney.pro.lifetime), with Restore purchases available and cancellation preserving selections.
