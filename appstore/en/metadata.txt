Description
Make room for the moments you want to keep. Pruney helps you review your photo library, choose what to delete, and reduce the size of supported photos and videos.

SEE YOUR LIBRARY
View accessible file sizes for photos, Live Photos, and videos in one clear overview. These measurements are not exact device storage usage or guaranteed space savings.

REVIEW WITH SWIPES
Swipe left to select deletion, right to keep, or up to resize. For Live Photos, the upward action makes them static. Undo a decision or apply an action to the remaining items. Reviewing does not change your files.

FILTER BEFORE YOU START
Choose media type, creation date, and file size. Combine conditions in a filter, or add alternative filters to build the review you need.

CHOOSE YOUR RESIZE LEVEL
Pick Light, Balanced, High, or Maximum for supported photos and videos. Photo levels retain 90%, 80%, 70%, or 50% of the original width and height, not file size. Video levels use a compatible smaller resolution. Supported photo formats are preserved. Live Photos can become ordinary photos without the motion effect.

YOU DECIDE WHEN TO APPLY
Review your selection before starting. Resizing and static conversion save a replacement and delete the original. Photos may request system confirmation. Deleted files remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days; empty that album yourself to free space immediately.

FREE TO START
Each session includes up to 50 deletions and 10 resize or static conversions. Keeping items is unlimited. Pruney Pro is an optional one-time lifetime purchase for unlimited actions, with no subscription.

Pruney works with the Photos access you grant, including limited access. Library analysis and file-size filtering do not download cloud-only resources. Unsupported media is excluded from review.
